## Overview

Ennismore is a hospitality company that builds lifestyle hotels and restaurants with strong personalities. Its portfolio includes The Hoxton, Mama Shelter and Mondrian. Each new restaurant needed a website that gave customers the essentials: the menus, opening hours, how to book, while feeling like an extension of the restaurant itself.

<Figure src={overviewPatatino} alt="Patatino restaurant interior" caption="Patatino restaurant in Edinburgh" />

## System Thinking

Every restaurant needed the same core information. So to save time, I built a modular design system: reusable components like headers, hero sections, menus, events, timetables, address blocks and footers, each adaptable to a different brand. The components stayed constant across all six sites, with variants added only where a restaurant needed something different. Once the team agreed on the structure and content, I moved to design.

## Applying the brands

Once the structure was agreed, I designed the six sites, following each brand's guidelines. A few elements had to be reworked to fit the web (a couple of the palettes were built for print and didn't pass contrast checks). Every site shared the same structure, but each kept its own personality.

### Patatino

Patatino is a lively Italian restaurant inspired by the Amalfi Coast, offering playful decor, theatrical service and shareable dishes. Patatino is located in the Hoxton Hotel in Edinburgh.

<Figure src={patatino} alt="Patatino website design" />

### Lora

Lora is based at the SLS Hotel in Barcelona. The restaurant blends authentic Middle Eastern and Mediterranean flavours with a contemporary twist.

<Figure src={lora} alt="Lora website design" />

### Alassio

Alassio is an Italian restaurant located at the Hoxton Hotel in Florence, specialising in grilled fish and seafood. Inspired by the Italian Riviera, the menu features top Italian ingredients and vibrant, celebratory service.

<Figure src={alassio} alt="Alassio website design" />

### Bar l'Anxova Divina

Bar l'Anxova Divina is an informal tapas bar that celebrates Spanish art and design and specialises in Catalan cuisine.

<Figure src={barLanxova} alt="Bar l'Anxova Divina website design" />

### Cosmico

Cosmico is a rooftop bar in Barcelona, serving cocktails, bites and poolside beats. It's set across two infinity pools, with panoramic views of the marina and skyline.

<Figure src={cosmico} alt="Cosmico website design" />

### Kyara

Kyara is a vibrant, conceptual hotel bar blending unconventional ingredients and classic cocktails.

<Figure src={kyara} alt="Kyara website design" />

## Final outcome

All six websites launched on schedule. The client can now extend the system themselves and build the next restaurant without having to rebuild from scratch.
